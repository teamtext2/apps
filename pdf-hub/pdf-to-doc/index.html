Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Details about export modes, OCR accuracy, and document privacy

What is the difference between "Visual Exact" and "Editable Flow"?
Visual Exact Mode (Image DOCX): Renders each PDF page into a high-DPI image and embeds it seamlessly into Word pages. Fonts, complex tables, stamps, signatures, and math formulas stay 100% identical without formatting shifts.

Editable Flow Mode (Text Reconstruction): Extracts headings, lines, and paragraphs into native editable Word text. If a page is a scanned image, the built-in AI OCR automatically extracts text so you can freely type, edit, and reformat.
What if my PDF document is a scan or photo?
Our app includes built-in AI OCR (Tesseract.js). In Editable Flow mode, scanned pages are automatically recognized and converted into editable paragraphs. In Visual Exact mode, scanned pages are preserved with crystal-clear visual quality.
Will the generated .docx file open in Microsoft Word and Google Docs?
Yes. The output strictly conforms to the international standard ECMA-376 Office Open XML (.docx), ensuring 100% compatibility with Microsoft Word, Office 365, Google Docs, WPS Office, and LibreOffice.
Are my documents uploaded to any remote server?
Never. 100% of PDF decoding, OCR recognition, and DOCX compilation runs locally inside your web browser. Your private documents never leave your personal computer or phone.
