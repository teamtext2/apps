What is Spac2 Pdf to Doc (Word)?

Spac2 Pdf to Doc (Word) is a free, browser-based document converter inside Spac2 PDF Hub that compiles PDF files into standard Microsoft Word OpenXML (.docx) documents compatible with Microsoft Word, Office 365, Google Docs, WPS Office, and LibreOffice.

Instead of uploading confidential contracts, financial reports, or academic papers to remote servers, Spac2 Pdf to Doc (Word) executes the entire decoding, optical recognition, and .docx packaging pipeline locally on your device using PDF.js, DOCX.js, and Tesseract.js.

Visual Exact (Image DOCX)

Renders each PDF page at 108, 150, or 200 DPI (JPEG/PNG) onto Word pages—100% immune to font loss, broken tables, stamps, or math formulas.

Editable Flow Reconstruction

Extracts paragraphs, lines, and auto-detected headings (H1/H2) into native editable Word text with Calibri, Arial, Times New Roman, or Segoe UI.

Built-In AI OCR & Fallback

Automatically runs Tesseract.js OCR on scanned pages (Auto-detect + 17 language packs) and falls back to high-res image embedding if needed.

Dual Live Preview & Copy

Inspect rendered pages in Visual Pages Preview or review/copy all extracted text in the Extracted Text Stream tab before downloading.

100% Client-Side & Zero File Caps: All PDF decoding, OCR recognition, and ECMA-376 .docx packaging happen locally in your browser—no accounts, no daily quotas, and no server uploads.

Choosing Between Visual Exact and Editable Flow

Spac2 Pdf to Doc (Word) provides two specialized conversion modes tailored to different document types:

  • Visual Exact (Image DOCX): Renders every selected PDF page at 108 DPI, 150 DPI (Recommended), or 200 DPI in JPEG or lossless PNG format and fits it onto Word pages. Ideal when preserving exact visual layout, signatures, stamps, tables, and formulas is critical.
  • Editable Flow (Text Reconstruction): Reconstructs native Word paragraphs and headings at ~75–90% layout fidelity so you can freely type, edit, delete, and reformat text. Supports Smart Paragraph Grouping, Auto-Detect Headings (H1/H2), and custom page ranges (all or e.g. 1-5, 8).
  • AI Neural Vision OCR: When converting scanned PDFs in Editable Flow mode, enable Auto-activate AI OCR with Auto Detect Language or choose a dedicated pack (English, Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, and more).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between "Visual Exact" and "Editable Flow"?
Visual Exact Mode renders each PDF page into a high-DPI image and embeds it into Word pages so fonts, complex tables, stamps, signatures, and math formulas stay 100% identical. Editable Flow Mode extracts headings, lines, and paragraphs into native editable Word text (and automatically runs AI OCR on scanned pages).
What if my PDF document is a scan or photo?
In Editable Flow mode, built-in Tesseract.js OCR automatically recognizes text from scanned pages using Auto Detect or your chosen language pack. If a page contains no extractable text, the optional image fallback embeds the high-resolution page image so no content is lost.
Will the generated .docx file open in Microsoft Word and Google Docs?
Yes. The compiled file strictly follows the ECMA-376 Office Open XML (.docx) standard, ensuring full compatibility with Microsoft Word, Office 365, Google Docs, WPS Office, and LibreOffice.
Are my documents uploaded to any remote server?
Never. 100% of PDF decoding, OCR recognition, and DOCX compilation runs locally inside your browser. Your private documents never leave your device.
