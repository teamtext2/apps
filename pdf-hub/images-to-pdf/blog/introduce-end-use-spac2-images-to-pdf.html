What is Spac2 PDF - Images to PDF?

Spac2 PDF - Images to PDF is a free, browser-based document builder inside Spac2 PDF Hub that turns photos, scans, and screenshots into unified, standardized PDF files. Whether you are assembling scanned receipts, ID photos, design mockups, or lecture slides, every image is compiled locally on your device.

Unlike cloud converters that upload your personal photos to remote servers, Spac2 PDF - Images to PDF runs the entire rendering and packaging pipeline in browser memory using HTML5 Canvas and pdf-lib—guaranteeing zero server uploads and instant compilation.

Multi-Format & Ctrl+V Paste

Supports JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG, and AVIF files—plus instant Ctrl+V clipboard paste for screenshots.

Drag, Sort & Rotate

Drag thumbnails or tap arrows to reorder pages, sort filenames A–Z, rotate individual pages or all images 90°, and preview fullscreen.

Custom Paper & Margins

Choose Fit (Same as image), A4, US Letter, A3, or Legal with Auto/Portrait/Landscape orientation and None/Small/Large margins.

100% Client-Side Privacy

No accounts, no file size caps, and no watermarks. All processing stays inside your local browser memory on desktop or mobile.

Smart Transparency Handling: When exporting compressed JPEG pages from transparent PNG or SVG files, Spac2 automatically fills a crisp white (#ffffff) backdrop so transparent regions never render as solid black.

PDF Layout & Quality Options Explained

The PDF Settings sidebar gives you full control over how your images are placed into the final document:

  • Page Size: Select Fit (Same as image) to match each PDF page to its image dimensions, or pick standard print formats (A4, US Letter, A3, Legal).
  • Page Orientation & Margins (for preset paper sizes): Switch between Auto (matches each image's aspect), Portrait, or Landscape, and add None, Small (5mm), or Large (12mm) margins.
  • Image Fit: Choose Fit Page (Preserve ratio) to keep proportions intact, or Fill Page to stretch to the margin edges.
  • Image Quality & Filename: Pick High (0.92), Medium (0.80), or Low (0.60) compression, and customize your output .pdf filename before compiling.

How to Convert Images to PDF: Step-by-Step

1

Select, Drop, or Paste Your Images

Open spac2.com/apps/pdf-hub/images-to-pdf/ and drag and drop your images onto the dropzone, click Select Image Files, or press Ctrl+V to paste screenshots directly from your clipboard.

2

Reorder, Rotate & Inspect Pages

Use the studio toolbar to Add more photos, Sort A-Z, or Rotate All 90°. On each card, drag to reorder (or tap ‹ / › on mobile), rotate individually, or click the eye icon to inspect fullscreen.

3

Configure PDF Settings

In the PDF Settings panel (tap PDF Setup on mobile to jump straight there), choose your Page Size, Orientation, Margins, Image Fit, Quality, and Output Filename.

4

Compile, Preview & Download

Click Convert Images to PDF. Once compiled, click Download PDF to save your file, click Preview to open it in a new browser tab, or click Edit / More to adjust settings and re-export.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are my photos uploaded to any server?
No. Spac2 PDF - Images to PDF runs 100% locally in your browser using HTML5 Canvas and pdf-lib. Your photos and generated PDF never leave your device.
Can I paste screenshots directly without saving them first?
Yes. Copy any image or screenshot to your clipboard and press Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V on macOS) anywhere on the page to add it straight to your PDF queue.
How do I keep each PDF page the exact same size as my image?
Keep Page Size set to Fit (Same as image) (default). Each PDF page will automatically match the exact dimensions of its corresponding image with zero margins.
Can I reorder or rotate images on a mobile phone?
Yes. Every image card includes touch-friendly ‹ and › buttons to move pages left or right, plus a quick rotate button and a sticky bottom convert dock on mobile screens.
